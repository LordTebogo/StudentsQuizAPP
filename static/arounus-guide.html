Learn to use Arounus

This guide is included for every learner. Read the steps below, watch a tutorial when one is available, or save these instructions as a document.

Open AROUNUS module

1. Find your learning space

  1. Sign in with your learner account.
  2. Choose your subjects when asked the first time. The AROUNUS guide is added automatically.
  3. Use Home for your learner workspace and Lessons to see videos for your subjects.

2. Take a quiz

  1. From your learner home, choose a subject and open a quiz.
  2. Answer each question. Your progress saves as you work.
  3. Submit when you are finished, then open My results & scripts to see your results and download your script.

3. Watch a video lesson

  1. Open Lessons, choose a module, and select a lesson.
  2. Press play. Use the player settings to choose quality; quality above 480p requires premium access.
  3. Answer the questions below the video and submit your work. You can also join the discussion.

4. Get help

  • If you cannot sign in, ask your lecturer or administrator to help recover your account.
  • If a subject is missing after your first selection, ask your lecturer or administrator to add it.
  • Use the Support & account recovery link for general help and privacy information.
