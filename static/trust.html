What we check — and what we don’t

Rooms need administrator approval before they appear publicly. That is a review for publication, not an inspection of the room or proof that the provider owns it. Other market listings can publish automatically.

Arounus does not independently verify marketplace providers’ identities, student status, ownership, qualifications, prices, condition or current availability. Contact details and descriptions are supplied by the provider. A published listing, rating, or featured placement is not an endorsement or a guarantee.

Confirm availability directly, inspect the offer and check who you are dealing with before paying. The reviewed SRC profiles described below relate to civic announcements only; they do not verify marketplace sellers.

How contacting a provider works

The contact number and website on a listing belong to its provider. You can use those details directly without an Arounus account. Phone, WhatsApp, email and website contact happens outside Arounus; those services may reveal your number or profile and have their own privacy rules.

Message provider asks you to sign in and sends a message to the provider’s Arounus inbox. These messages are linked to your account and are not anonymous. Find replies through My market messages on the market homepage. Providers choose when to reply; Arounus does not guarantee a response.

Arounus connects you with providers. It does not collect marketplace deposits, hold payments in escrow, guarantee a transaction or provide buyer protection. Agree viewing, delivery and payment directly with the provider. Never send passwords, login codes, ID documents or card details in a message.

Report a stale or suspicious listing

Use Report on a listing to flag an unavailable room or item, old details, misleading content or a suspected scam. You can also paste its listing link here. Reporting does not require an account.

Reports enter an administrator-only review queue. Administrators can remove a room’s approval or withdraw a listing after reviewing it. A report does not automatically take a listing down, and no review time or outcome is guaranteed. The report text is not shown to the provider or other visitors.

For urgent concerns, contact the Arounus team with the listing link and any report reference. If a listing has disappeared, send its link to support. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Privacy

Listing reports store the listing reference, reason, details and review status. They are visible only to administrators. A hashed network identifier is stored to limit report spam; the raw network address is not stored with the report. Do not include sensitive personal information in report text.

Arounus collects the account details you provide, learning activity needed to deliver quizzes and results, and optional profile information. This information is used to operate the platform, support learning, protect accounts, and communicate within the services you choose.

To understand app traffic, Arounus uses a random first-party browser cookie lasting up to one year. Activity times and a hashed browser identifier are stored to count current, hourly and daily visitors. Traffic records do not include names, account details, IP addresses, page addresses or page contents. Only administrators can view aggregate visitor totals. Clearing cookies or using another device may count as another visitor.

Do not upload sensitive personal information to profile or community fields. You may ask the platform administrator to correct or remove your account information, subject to academic record requirements.

Terms of use

Use Arounus for lawful learning and community activities. Keep your password private, submit your own academic work, respect other users, and do not misuse private content or attempt to access another account.

Learning materials and assessment content remain subject to their authors’ and institution’s rules. Community and market content may be moderated or removed to protect users.

Privacy requests

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you rights concerning your personal information. Contact the Arounus team or use the private inbox to ask an administrator what information Arounus holds about you, or to request a correction or deletion. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

For independent advice or a complaint, visit the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Staying safe on the local market

Check the person and the offer independently before you pay or share personal information.

  • View a room in person or by live video before paying a deposit. Ask for a written agreement and verify who is entitled to rent it.
  • For items, meet in a public place during the day where possible. Inspect the item before paying.
  • Be cautious of pressure to pay quickly, unusually low prices, or requests to use untraceable payment methods.
  • Keep a record of messages, receipts, and the listing if something goes wrong.

Use Report a listing to flag stale details or suspicious behaviour without signing in. Keep the listing link and report reference.
