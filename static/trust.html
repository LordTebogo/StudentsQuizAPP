Privacy

Arounus collects the account details you provide, learning activity needed to deliver quizzes and results, and optional profile information. This information is used to operate the platform, support learning, protect accounts, and communicate within the services you choose.

Do not upload sensitive personal information to profile or community fields. You may ask the platform administrator to correct or remove your account information, subject to academic record requirements.

Terms of use

Use Arounus for lawful learning and campus activities. Keep your password private, submit your own academic work, respect other users, and do not misuse private content or attempt to access another account.

Learning materials and assessment content remain subject to their authors’ and institution’s rules. Community and market content may be moderated or removed to protect users.

Support & account recovery

If you cannot sign in, contact your lecturer or Arounus administrator and provide your username or registered email address. Never send your password. An administrator can verify your identity and reset access.

If you can sign in, use the private inbox to contact an administrator about account, privacy, or marketplace concerns. Include the listing title and campus when reporting a listing. Never send your password or bank card details.

Privacy requests

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you rights concerning your personal information. Use the private inbox to ask an administrator what information Arounus holds about you, or to request a correction or deletion. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

For independent advice or a complaint, visit the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Staying safe on the campus market

Check the person and the offer independently before you pay or share personal information.

  • View a room in person or by live video before paying a deposit. Ask for a written agreement and verify who is entitled to rent it.
  • For items, meet in a public place during the day where possible. Inspect the item before paying.
  • Be cautious of pressure to pay quickly, unusually low prices, or requests to use untraceable payment methods.
  • Keep a record of messages, receipts, and the listing if something goes wrong.

To report a suspicious listing, send its title, campus, and reason to an administrator using the private inbox.
