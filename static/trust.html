Trust centre

Clear, safe learning

NucleoCampus is the Bioscientist learning platform for learners and teaching staff, with optional community and market spaces.

Private accountsLearner and tutor workspaces require sign-in. Access tokens stay in the current browser session and are cleared when you sign out.
Controlled academic accessTutors review work assigned to their students. Administrative tools use protected access.
Verified civic profilesSRC profiles are reviewed before they can present announcements as verified communications.

Privacy

NucleoCampus collects the account details you provide, learning activity needed to deliver quizzes and results, and optional profile information. This information is used to operate the platform, support learning, protect accounts, and communicate within the services you choose.

Do not upload sensitive personal information to profile or community fields. You may ask the platform administrator to correct or remove your account information, subject to academic record requirements.

Terms of use

Use NucleoCampus for lawful learning and campus activities. Keep your password private, submit your own academic work, respect other users, and do not misuse private content or attempt to access another account.

Learning materials and assessment content remain subject to their authors’ and institution’s rules. Community and market content may be moderated or removed to protect users.

Support & account recovery

If you cannot sign in, contact your lecturer or NucleoCampus administrator and provide your username or registered email address. Never send your password. An administrator can verify your identity and reset access.

For privacy, access, verification, or technical questions, use the available support contact.
