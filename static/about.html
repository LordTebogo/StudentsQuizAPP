THE STORY BEHIND AROUNUS

A purpose
close to home.

I’m Tebogo Manyubele, the founder of Arounus. I’m from Limpopo, and I believe that learning, finding a place to stay and getting everyday essentials should be easier.

Read my story ↓Support Arounus ↗
WHY I STARTED

I saw the struggle.
I wanted to help.

In Limpopo, I saw how difficult it could be for people to find an affordable room near a college or university. Looking for accommodation often meant a lot of searching, uncertainty and frustration. I decided to build Arounus to help make that process simpler.

I also want to help learners and students prepare, build confidence and pass their assessments. Through tutorials, practice quizzes and connections with tutors, Arounus brings learning support into one place.

That same idea extends beyond education. Whether you are around a company, school, college, university, mine or neighbourhood, Arounus is here to help you find useful services, shop locally and connect with the people and opportunities around you.
