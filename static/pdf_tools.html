Conversion notes

PDF to Word/PowerPoint: Every PDF page is placed visually into the Office file, preserving the appearance reliably.

Word/PowerPoint to PDF: Text, tables, pictures, and slide content are exported. Advanced animations, embedded media, charts, and Office-only effects may be simplified.

Password-protected documents are not processed.

Privacy: Files are processed for your download and are not added to quizzes, profiles, or the document library.
